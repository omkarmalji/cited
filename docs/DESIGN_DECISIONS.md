# Design refinements

The original design.md remains the initial reference. Subsequent owner feedback overrides its monochrome and hero-serif requirements.

- Background: the exact ThreeUI Predictive Arc canvas fills the entire hero, behind the content container, on desktop and mobile. No vendor source or configured motion/color props are changed.
- Typography: hero, section headings and body use the existing Inter family. JetBrains Mono remains for evidence, counts and source receipts.
- Color: dark violet page and panel surfaces, lavender primary buttons and selected headline text add emphasis. Status colors retain their meaning and visible labels.
- Density: remove duplicate header actions and decorative feature illustrations. Group question rows into a single divided list; use compact fix rows and collapse optional account-saving controls.
- Motion: preserve GSAP entry and section reveals. Reduced-motion mode displays content directly and omits the decorative canvas.

The functional evidence, confirmation and approval flows remain central: five runs per question, fetched citation links, explicit fact confirmation, rejected unsupported edits and owner approval before publishing.

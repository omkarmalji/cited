Target length: 2 minutes 55 seconds. Record after deployment and live-provider acceptance checks pass. Public YouTube upload and feedback form remain submission tasks.

Show the landing hero. Say: "Customers are asking AI who to visit. Local owners have no easy way to know whether they are recommended. Cited shows what happens, why a rival wins, and what to fix, in plain language."

## 0:20 to 1:15 | Check

Explain: "We fetch sources with Tavily, confirm the owner’s facts, then ask eight questions five times each through NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory."

## 1:15 to 1:55 | Report and evidence

Show five dots and independent answers. Open a citation. Explain broad versus niche outlook. Open the zero-mention Spoke Studio example: "A disappointing score still gives useful next steps. These examples are fictional saved data. The live flow does not substitute samples."

## 1:55 to 2:35 | Fix and approval

Open page editor. Show confirmed facts. Add "Open 24 hours" and demonstrate approval being blocked. Restore the draft, approve, and open the hosted page. Show other packs and review QR card with a confirmed review URL. Explain that nothing goes public without approval.

## 2:35 to 2:55 | Technology and impact

"Nano runs the repeated recommendations; Super diagnoses sources; Ultra checks the fix plan. Tavily searches and extracts pages. Scores are Nemotron plus web search, not live ChatGPT. Cited gives small businesses practical, evidence-backed actions without pretending to guarantee rankings."
